Account & Data Deletion Request

App Name: Ridonova Driver (Ridonova Captain)

Company: Ridonova Technologies

At Ridonova, we respect your privacy and provide you with full control over your account and personal data. If you wish to delete your Ridonova Driver account and associated data, please review the methods available below.

Important Note: Deleting your account is permanent. All profile details, vehicle registrations, KYC documents, and earnings ledger histories will be permanently removed or anonymized in accordance with legal and financial compliance retention requirements.

Method 1: Delete Account via the Ridonova Driver App

You can request account closure directly from within the mobile app:

  • Open the Ridonova Driver App on your mobile device.
  • Navigate to the Profile tab from the bottom navigation bar.
  • Go to Captain Support & Help.
  • Raise a support ticket with the category "Other Inquiries / Feedback" or "Account Deletion Request".
  • Our support team will verify your identity and process your account deletion request within 24 to 48 hours.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can send an account deletion request directly to our support team:

Email: support@ridonova.com

Phone: 7978800300

Please include in your email:

  • Your Registered Full Name
  • Your Registered Mobile Number (7978800300)
  • Reason for account deletion

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained?

  • Data Deleted: Your personal profile, login credentials, device tokens, temporary session logs, and active ride location history.
  • Data Retained (As Required by Law): Financial transaction logs, payout histories, and tax compliance records are retained for the legally mandated period (e.g., up to 5 years) for accounting, anti-fraud, and tax auditing purposes.
