Privacy Policy

App Name: Ridonova Driver (Ridonova Captain)

Effective Date: October 17, 2026

Ridonova ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Ridonova Driver Mobile Application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

Commitment to Privacy: We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal and financial information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

To provide reliable ride-matching, secure navigation, and daily earnings payouts, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, phone number (7978800300), email address (support@ridonova.com).
  • KYC & Verification Documents: Government-issued identification such as Aadhar Card, PAN Card, and Driving License.
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle registration number, make, model, fuel type, RC document, and vehicle insurance details.
  • Precise Location Data: Real-time GPS location data is collected while the app is running in the foreground and background when you are online on duty. This is essential for matching nearby ride requests, GPS navigation, and calculating trip distances.
  • Financial & Banking Information: Bank account numbers, IFSC codes, and UPI IDs used for processing instant cashouts and earnings payouts.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Device model, operating system version, battery level status, and network connectivity status to optimize app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To verify driver identity, KYC documents, and vehicle registration compliance.
  • To connect drivers with nearby passengers seeking rides and provide turn-by-turn navigation.
  • To calculate fares, process instant cashouts, and manage daily incentive goals.
  • To deduct platform commissions and maintain transparent ledger transactions.
  • To provide 24/7 captain support, handle support tickets, and resolve fare/toll disputes.
  • To send critical operational notifications, ride alerts, and duty status updates.

3. Location Data Usage

Ridonova Driver collects location data to enable ride matching, navigation, and distance tracking even when the app is closed or not in use (background location), provided you have toggled your duty status to "Online". You can disable background location tracking at any time by going offline in the app settings or adjusting your device permission settings.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only under the following circumstances:

  • Payment Gateways & Banking Partners: To process secure earnings transfers and instant bank payouts.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When required by law, court order, or government regulation to ensure public safety and fraud prevention.

5. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We implement industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for data in transit and secure database storage protocols to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You can request account deletion or data removal by contacting our support team or submitting a request through the app's support ticket system. Upon account termination, we retain transactional records and financial ledger histories as required by tax and financial compliance laws.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date.
